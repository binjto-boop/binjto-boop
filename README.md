Press <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/binjto-boop/binjto-boop/main/buttons/kbd-opta.svg" align="absmiddle" alt="Option A"> over any word on your Mac screen. The meaning
opens right where you are reading, and closes when you are done.

It reads pixels, not text fields. A scanned PDF works the same as a web page,
and so does a screenshot or a subtitle. Whatever you look up gets saved with the
sentence it came from.

Ten study languages. Free while in beta.
